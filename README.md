# KINETIC PADS

Kinetic Pads is a Fabric Minecraft mod for the version 26.2 which adds three variants of Launch Pads to Minecraft. It includes smoke and sound effects along with fall damage protection when landing.

## It includes:
1. Three Launch Pad tiers : Soft, Normal, Hard
2. Smoke and Sound effects
3. Temporary Fall Damage Protection
4. Thin carpet like block so there is no need to jump on it, just walk over it and the player will get launched.
5. Can only be place on top of a block
6. Survival friendly crafting recipe

## Values Of The Pads
1. Kinetic Pad Soft : Launch Power = 0.6, Fall Damage Protection = 3 seconds, Main Item used in the recipe : Copper
2. Kinetic Pad : Launch Power = 1.2, Fall Damage Protection = 5 seconds, Main Item used in the recipe : Iron
3. Kinetic Pad Hard : Launch Power = 2.0, Fall Damage Protection = 8 seconds, Main item used in the recipe : Diamond

## Crafting Recipe

M - Copper Ingot (Soft) / Iron Ingot (Normal) / Diamond (Hard)

W - Wind Charge

R - Redstone

Recipe : 

MMM

WMW

RRR
